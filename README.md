# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator that computes simple interest and total maturity amount from principal, annual interest rate, and time period.

## Formula

$$\text{Simple Interest} (SI) = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$

$$\text{Total Amount} (A) = P + SI$$

- **P**: Principal amount (initial amount)
- **R**: Annual rate of interest (in percentage)
- **T**: Time period (in years)

## Example
- **Principal (P):** 1000
- **Rate (R):** 5%
- **Time (T):** 3 years
- **Simple Interest (SI):** 150
- **Total Amount (A):** 1150
